# Refund and Return Request Call Handling SOP

This SOP specifies the precise, step-by-step procedure frontline agents must follow when a customer calls to request a return, replacement, or monetary refund.

---

### [REF-S1] Authenticate Order and Customer Identity
- **Description:** Collect and verify the ShopEase Order ID and the last 4 digits of the registered mobile phone number prior to discussing order or payment details.
- **What Good Looks Like:** The agent confirms both values in the CRM portal before mentioning what items were purchased or amounts paid.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"I will be glad to assist with your refund. First, could you please confirm your Order ID and the last four digits of your registered mobile number?"*
  - *"To protect your account details, may I have your Order ID and the last four digits of your contact number?"*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** Yes

---

### [REF-S2] Determine Return Window Eligibility by Category
- **Description:** Check order delivery date against item category window (7 calendar days for electronics, 14 calendar days for non-electronics).
- **What Good Looks Like:** The agent explicitly checks the delivery timestamp in the CRM and articulates the applicable category window clearly.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"I see this smartwatch was delivered on October 10th. Because electronics have a 7-day return window, your request today is fully eligible."*
  - *"This apparel item was delivered 6 days ago. It falls well within our 14-day return window for non-electronic items."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** Yes

---

### [REF-S3] Inspect Reason for Return and Evidence
- **Description:** Identify reason for return (damaged, defective, wrong size, or dissatisfied) and request photo proof if damaged/DOA.
- **What Good Looks Like:** If customer claims damage or wrong product, agent confirms whether it was reported within 48 hours and verifies unboxing photos.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"Could you please let me know the specific issue you encountered with the product?"*
  - *"Since you mentioned the screen was cracked upon delivery, could you upload a quick photo of the outer box and device through the ShopEase app?"*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** No

---

### [REF-S4] Verify Monetary Approval Threshold Limit
- **Description:** Check if the requested refund or concession amount is within the frontline agent limit of Rs 5,000.
- **What Good Looks Like:** If the refund is Rs 5,000 or less, the agent approves immediately. If above Rs 5,000, the agent informs the customer that supervisor sign-off is required.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"The refund amount of Rs 2,499 is within my authorization limit, and I have approved the return immediately."*
  - *"Because the refund amount is Rs 12,000, company policy requires a quick supervisor sign-off. I will initiate that right now for you."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** Yes

---

### [REF-S5] Communicate Refund Method and Payment Destination
- **Description:** Inform customer that refunds return strictly to original payment source, or via NEFT bank transfer for Cash on Delivery (COD) orders.
- **What Good Looks Like:** Clear communication preventing customer expectation of cash payouts or transfers to unverified third-party wallets.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"Your refund will be returned directly to the HDFC credit card used during purchase."*
  - *"Since this was a Cash on Delivery order, our system will transfer the refund via NEFT to your verified bank account."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** Yes

---

### [REF-S6] Articulate the 5-7 Business Day SLA Timeline
- **Description:** Clearly state that the refund will process within 5 to 7 business days following reverse pickup and warehouse inspection.
- **What Good Looks Like:** The agent quotes the exact 5-7 business day timeline without promising unrealistic instant or same-day turnaround.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"Once the courier collects the parcel, the refund will be credited to your account within 5 to 7 business days."*
  - *"Please allow 5 to 7 business days from the pickup date for the funds to reflect in your bank statement."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** No

---

### [REF-S7] Generate Reverse Pickup and Share Reference Ticket
- **Description:** Book reverse logistics pickup slot, generate service ticket reference, and provide pickup instructions.
- **What Good Looks like:** The agent provides the courier pickup timeframe (48-72 hours) and reminds the customer to retain all original packaging and accessories.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"I have scheduled your return pickup for tomorrow. Your return tracking number is RTN-55412. Please keep the item packed with all tags and original box."*
  - *"The courier will visit within 48 hours to collect the item. Please keep the original invoice and box ready for handoff."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** No

---

## Common Mistakes & Deviations
1. **Promising Unapproved Instant Refunds:** Telling callers "money will reach your account in 30 minutes" instead of the official 5 to 7 business days (`REF-S6`).
2. **Ignoring Electronics Return Window:** Accepting returns on electronics delivered 10+ days prior without directing to brand warranty (`REF-S2`).
3. **Approving Refunds Above Rs 5,000 Unilaterally:** Issuing high-value refunds without supervisor escalation (`REF-S4`).
4. **Promising Cash Handover on COD:** Misleading customers by saying courier delivery agents will hand over physical cash during reverse pickup (`REF-S5`).
5. **Skipping 48-Hour Damage Check:** Accepting claims of physical transit breakage reported weeks after delivery without photos (`REF-S3`).
