# General Customer Service Call Flow SOP

This standard operating procedure defines the end-to-end conversation structure required on every inbound and outbound customer support interaction at ShopEase.

---

### [GEN-01] Professional Greeting & Identity Introduction
- **Description:** Greet the customer with high energy, state company name, introduce yourself by name, and inquire how you may assist.
- **What Good Looks Like:** The agent opens within 2 seconds of call connection with a polite, welcoming tone, introducing both the brand and representative name.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"Thank you for calling ShopEase. My name is Rahul. How may I assist you today?"*
  - *"Good morning! Welcome to ShopEase customer support, my name is Priya. Who do I have the pleasure of speaking with?"*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** No

---

### [GEN-02] Mandatory Call Recording Disclosure
- **Description:** Provide the required statutory notification informing the customer that the interaction is being recorded for quality and compliance.
- **What Good Looks Like:** The disclosure is clearly articulated within the first two conversation turns without mumbling or skipping.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"Please note that this call is being recorded for quality monitoring and training purposes."*
  - *"Before we begin, please be advised that our call is recorded for quality assurance."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** Yes

---

### [GEN-03] Caller Identification & Two-Factor Verification
- **Description:** Authenticate caller identity by requesting Order ID and the last 4 digits of the registered mobile phone number before revealing any account data.
- **What Good Looks Like:** The agent systematically validates both verification data points before disclosing addresses, order contents, or monetary info.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"To ensure your account security, may I please have your Order ID and the last four digits of your registered mobile number?"*
  - *"For verification purposes, could you please confirm your ShopEase Order ID along with the last four digits of your phone number?"*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** Yes

---

### [GEN-04] Active Listening & Issue Clarification
- **Description:** Allow the customer to describe their concern without interrupting, then paraphrase the core issue to confirm mutual understanding.
- **What Good Looks Like:** The agent maintains silence while the customer speaks, takes notes, and summarizes the specific problem back to the customer.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"I understand that your package has not arrived and you would like to track the courier status. Is that correct?"*
  - *"If I heard correctly, you received the wrong shoe size and would like to arrange an exchange. Let me check that for you."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** No

---

### [GEN-05] Empathy & Ownership Statement
- **Description:** Express sincere empathy for the customer's inconvenience and offer personal reassurance that you will take ownership of the solution.
- **What Good Looks Like:** Empathetic acknowledgment that avoids robotic stock phrases, validating the customer's frustration.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"I completely understand how frustrating it is to wait for an order, and I am personally going to sort this out for you."*
  - *"I am truly sorry for the trouble this has caused you today. You have reached the right person, and I will resolve this."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** No

---

### [GEN-06] Accurate Policy Explanation & Solution Execution
- **Description:** State company terms, timelines, and resolutions strictly grounded in verified ShopEase policy documents.
- **What Good Looks Like:** Explaining exact delivery or refund windows (e.g., 5-7 business days for refund, 7 days for electronics) without making out-of-policy promises.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"Per our policy, since your wireless earbuds are within the 7-day electronics return window, I have scheduled a reverse pickup for tomorrow."*
  - *"Our standard delivery timeline is 3 to 7 business days, and your parcel is currently out for delivery today."*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** Yes

---

### [GEN-07] Additional Assistance & Next Steps Summary
- **Description:** Summarize the exact resolution agreed upon, reference ticket numbers, and proactively ask if there are any other questions.
- **What Good Looks Like:** Clear recap of actions taken, tracking reference shared, and an open invitation for secondary questions.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"To recap, I have initiated return ticket #SR-8921. You will receive an SMS confirmation. Is there anything else I can help you with today?"*
  - *"Your replacement order has been booked. Aside from this, is there anything else I can look into for you?"*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** No

---

### [GEN-08] Professional Branded Sign-Off
- **Description:** Conclude the interaction with a warm, professional closing mentioning ShopEase.
- **What Good Looks Like:** Courteous parting statement thanking the customer, waiting for the customer to disconnect first.
- **Example Phrases:**
  - *"Thank you for contacting ShopEase. We appreciate your business and hope you have a great day ahead!"*
  - *"Thank you for your time today. It was a pleasure assisting you at ShopEase. Goodbye!"*
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Critical:** No

---

## Common Mistakes & Deviations
1. **Skipping Recording Disclosure:** Proceeding into conversation without reciting the call recording disclaimer (`GEN-02`).
2. **Premature Account Data Disclosure:** Revealing customer address or past purchase details before verifying Order ID and phone digits (`GEN-03`).
3. **Interrupting Customer Monologue:** Speaking over an agitated customer within the first 10 seconds of their issue explanation.
4. **False Timeline Promises:** Promising "instant refund in 1 hour" or "same-day delivery" contrary to official 5-7 business day and 3-7 day policy standards (`GEN-06`).
5. **Abrupt Call Termination:** Hanging up immediately after solving the issue without offering recap or asking for additional needs (`GEN-07`, `GEN-08`).
